PyroWatch aggregates NASA FIRMS satellite heat detections, NWS weather, EPA AirNow and NIFC WFIGS incident data. A detection is a heat anomaly, not a confirmed wildfire — it may also come from prescribed burns, gas flares or other heat sources, and clouds, smoke and satellite passes can make detections appear or vanish. Scores are a context indicator only — not an official warning and never a substitute for emergency guidance from local authorities.
